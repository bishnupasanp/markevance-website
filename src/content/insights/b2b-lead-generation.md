# The Current B2B Growth Problem

Many growing B2B businesses in Nepal have a great product, successful case studies, and capable delivery teams. But they often struggle when it comes to systematic customer acquisition. Growth becomes dependent on personal networks, referrals, and founder-led sales.

When marketing is finally attempted, it is often reduced to social media activities that generate noise, rather than a structured system that generates pipeline.
